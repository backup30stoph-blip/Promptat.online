# Crawling & Indexing Matrix

## Target Settings
Allows administrators to configure crawlers instructions:
* **Allow Indexing**: Global toggle for robots meta `noindex`
* **Content Types Matrix**: Configures toggles individually for:
  - Pages
  - Prompts
  - Blogs
  - Skills
  - Video Concepts
  - Categories
  - Authors
  - Collections
* **Toggles**: Selectable between `Index` or `NoIndex`, and `Follow` or `NoFollow`.
