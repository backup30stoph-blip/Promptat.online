# Sitemap Settings

## Target Capabilities
* **Auto-generation**: Recalculates node lists for blogs, skills, categories, and video assets.
* **Content Nodes**: Expose toggles to include images, videos, tags, authors, or categories.
* **Pings**: Functions to notify Google and Bing search consoles about updates.
