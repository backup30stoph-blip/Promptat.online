## 9. Implementation Notes

- **H1/H2 mapping** — make sure the Display Headline in Section 2 is your actual `<h1>`, and every "Section Heading" in Sections 3–7 is a real `<h2>`, not just styled text. Keyword-rich copy sitting in a `<div>` with no semantic heading tag gets materially less ranking credit than the same words in a proper heading element.
- **Don't repeat the identical headline verbatim across pages.** The hero headline here ("Discover Gemini Image Prompts, Claude SEO Skills & Viral Video Concepts") should NOT also be reused as, say, the `/prompts` library page's H1 — give that page its own focus_keyword-aligned H1 per Skill 13, or you create the exact cannibalization problem Skill 13 Section 7 warns against, just between your own pages.
- **"Nano banana prompts" is a slang/nickname term** (referring to Gemini's image-generation model) — keep it in quotes or lowercase casual usage as shown, since forcing it into formal/capitalized headings can read oddly; informal placement in the sub-headline, trending tags, and newsletter line (as done above) matches how people actually search it.
- **Category rename flag**: renaming the `SEO` category to `Claude SEO Skills` (Section 3) changes its slug per Skill 13 — if that category is already published/indexed, follow the Skill 13 Section 6 process (301 redirect, not a silent slug edit) rather than just relabeling it.
- **Cross-check `/videos` copy**: Section 6 above standardizes on "Video Concepts" over the old "Video Blueprints" — if your actual `/videos` route (Skill 04) and footer (Skill 15, "Video Blueprints" label) still say "Blueprints," pick one term site-wide and update the other, otherwise you're splitting keyword signal between two phrases for the same content type.
